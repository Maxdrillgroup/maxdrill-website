DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Better decisions begin before execution.

Decision Intelligence combines structured reasoning, data, behavioural understanding and technology to improve the quality of choices under uncertainty.

MAXDRILL FRAMEWORK

Think. Analyse. Decide. Execute. Learn.

01 THINK

Define the real decision.

Clarify objectives, constraints, time horizon and acceptable risk before taking action.

02 ANALYSE

Use evidence and context.

Combine data with market structure, uncertainty and scenario thinking.

03 DECIDE

Choose with discipline.

Separate conviction from impulse and document the reasoning behind the decision.

04 EXECUTE

Act consistently.

Apply predefined rules, risk limits and operational discipline.

05 LEARN

Review the process, not only the outcome.

Distinguish good decisions from lucky outcomes and continuously improve the framework.
