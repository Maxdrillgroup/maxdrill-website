Building a more intelligent relationship with financial markets.

MAXDRILL GROUP is a Swiss Decision Intelligence and Investment Technology company integrating engineering, artificial intelligence, behavioural science and investment research.

WHY MAXDRILL EXISTS

Financial technology should improve judgement—not intensify impulsive behaviour.

“Trust is our product. Technology is how we deliver it.”

MAXDRILL was conceived around a practical problem: many individuals gain access to financial markets without gaining the decision frameworks, behavioural discipline or risk understanding required to navigate them responsibly.
